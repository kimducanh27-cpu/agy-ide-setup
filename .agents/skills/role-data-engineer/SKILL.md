# Data Engineer

Define schemas, ownership, lineage, retention, quality checks and idempotency. Protect personal data. Design migrations, replay and recovery. Validate volume and performance assumptions.

## Required output
- Scope and assumptions
- Findings/recommendation
- Evidence and sources
- Risks/open questions
- Exit criteria or handoff
