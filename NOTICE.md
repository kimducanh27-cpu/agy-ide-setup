# Notices and references

APES is an independently authored toolkit. Its role-based harness design was informed by public descriptions and the architecture of [vietanhdo/harness-skeleton](https://github.com/vietanhdo/harness-skeleton). APES does not vendor that repository's source code, role skill files, scripts, or assets. Consult the upstream repository's `LICENSE` before copying or redistributing any of its content.

Antigravity-native paths and formats are based on Google's official documentation:

- https://antigravity.google/docs/rules/
- https://antigravity.google/docs/skills
- https://antigravity.google/docs/customization-discovery

Repository authors should re-check official docs when Antigravity changes its customization behavior.
